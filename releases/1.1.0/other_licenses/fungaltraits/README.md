# Words of another source: FungalTraits

16 characters and 260 value options of the list are trait names and trait values of FungalTraits. They have
identifiers of the Mushroom Character Vocabulary, and their words stay the authors'.

Credit: Põlme S. et al. 2020. FungalTraits: a user-friendly traits database of fungi and fungus-like stramenopiles. Fungal Diversity 105: 1-16. doi:10.1007/s13225-020-00466-2. Table S1, Traits of genera.

The words are the authors'. They are shown as written and are not corrected here; a correction is theirs to make.
