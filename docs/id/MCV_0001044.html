Definitions

  • related: Kauffman 1918, The Agaricaceae of Michigan, CONIFER, p. 882
    mostly evergreen trees bearing cones.
    CONIFER defines the trees, not the forest; CONIFEROUS and FRONDOSE describe the forest.
