Definitions

  • the structure it describes: Kauffman 1918, The Agaricaceae of Michigan, HYPHAE, p. 888
    plural of hypha; same as mycelium, composing also the fruit-body.
    Record C00665; public domain in the United States (published 1918); read from the page image (scan leaf 924), 2026-11-23
  • the structure it describes: Kauffman 1918, The Agaricaceae of Michigan, MYCELIUM, p. 891
    came as hyphae; the vegetative part of a fungus consisting of microscopic threads usually with cross-walls to form the cells which contain the living protoplasm.
    Reached through 'same as mycelium' in the entry HYPHAE. 'came' is as printed; evidently 'same'.
