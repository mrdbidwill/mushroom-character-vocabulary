Definitions

  • defines: Kauffman 1918, The Agaricaceae of Michigan, SECEDE, p. 894
    (of gills), when at first attached to stem, i. e. adnate or adnexed, but separating from it later.
    'Seceding' is another form of the word SECEDE.
