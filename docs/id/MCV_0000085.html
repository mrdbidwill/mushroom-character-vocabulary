Definitions

  • the quality it records: Kauffman 1918, The Agaricaceae of Michigan, BULBOUS, p. 881
    (of stem), enlarged at base. (See also “abruptly-bulbous”, clavate-bulbous and round-bulbous.)
    The character records the type of a bulbous stem base.
