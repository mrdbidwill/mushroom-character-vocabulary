Definitions

  • related: Kauffman 1918, The Agaricaceae of Michigan, FIBROUS, p. 885
    (of flesh of stem), composed of toughish, string-like tissue.
    The entry is for the flesh of the stem, not its surface. 'Fibrose' is another form of the word.
