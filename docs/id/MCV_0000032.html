Definitions

  • related: Kauffman 1918, The Agaricaceae of Michigan, FIBRILLOSE, p. 885
    (of surface of cap and stem), provided with fibrils or clusters of small fibres composed of hyphae.
    The character records the color of the fibrils; the entry defines a surface provided with fibrils.
