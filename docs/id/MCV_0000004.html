Definitions

  • defines: Kauffman 1918, The Agaricaceae of Michigan, GILLS, p. 887
    the knife-blade-like structures on the underside of the pileus; lamellae; collectively, the hymenophore.
    Record D00007; public domain in the United States (published 1918); read from the page image, sources/kauffman_1918_pages/page_887_leaf923.jpg, 2026-11-23
  • cross-reference: Kauffman 1918, The Agaricaceae of Michigan, LAMELLAE, p. 889
    plural of lamella; same as gills.
    Record D00008; public domain in the United States (published 1918); read from the page image, sources/kauffman_1918_pages/page_889_leaf925.jpg, 2026-11-23
