Definitions

  • defines: Kauffman 1918, The Agaricaceae of Michigan, MARGINATE-DEPRESSED, p. 890
    (of bulb), provided with a narrow, circular, horizontal platform on the upper side.
    Record V00356; public domain in the United States (published 1918); read from the page image (scan leaf 926), 2026-11-23
  • related: Kauffman 1918, The Agaricaceae of Michigan, MARGINATE, p. 890
    (of pileus), with a distinctly marked border; (of bulb), with a circular ridge on the exterior upper angle where the universal veil was attached.
    Kauffman treats marginate and marginate-depressed as two forms of bulb.
    Record V00357; public domain in the United States (published 1918); read from the page image (scan leaf 926), 2026-11-23
