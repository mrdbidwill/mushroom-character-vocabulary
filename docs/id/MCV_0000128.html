Definitions

  • the structure it describes: Kauffman 1918, The Agaricaceae of Michigan, PELLICLE, p. 892
    same as cuticle, sometimes thought of as thinner and more definite.
    Record C00126; public domain in the United States (published 1918); read from the page image (scan leaf 928), 2026-11-23
  • the structure it describes: Kauffman 1918, The Agaricaceae of Michigan, CUTICLE, p. 883
    (of pileus or stem), a differentiated thin layer of hyphae on the surface; same as pellicle.
    Reached through 'same as cuticle' in the entry PELLICLE.
    Record C00127; public domain in the United States (published 1918); read from the page image (scan leaf 919), 2026-11-23
  • the quality it records: Kauffman 1918, The Agaricaceae of Michigan, GELATINOUS, p. 887
    jelly-like, applied to tissue whose hyphae become partially dissolved and glutinous in wet weather and when mounted in water under the microscope appear more transparent and wider, loosening from one another.
    Record C00128; public domain in the United States (published 1918); read from the page image (scan leaf 923), 2026-11-23
  • the quality it records: Kauffman 1918, The Agaricaceae of Michigan, SEPARABLE, p. 895
    (of cuticle, pellicle, etc.), not adnate.
    The entry names the pellicle, which is the cap's.
    Record C00129; public domain in the United States (published 1918); read from the page image (scan leaf 931), 2026-11-23
